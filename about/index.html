About

Hi, I'm Jacob.

I play Commander, and I like making my own versions of the cards in my decks. This is where I put them.

Each deck has its own set of frames. Magus Lucea Kane looks like a box of old playing cards, with Venetian engravings, Paris fortune-telling cards and Victorian card backs. Truthsayer is all cyanotype blue. Be'lakor uses summoning circles and burned grimoire pages.

Besides the decks there are a few small series and single cards, mostly me trying out an idea for a frame.

The art is mostly old paintings, prints and engravings. Every card has a credit line with the artist and the name of the work.
