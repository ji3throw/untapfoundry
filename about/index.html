About

Hi, I'm Jacob.

Untap Foundry is where I remake the Magic cards in my Commander decks, one card at a time, in frames of my own.

Each deck gets its own look. The Magus Lucea Kane deck is dressed as a box of antique playing cards: Venetian engravings, Paris fortune-telling decks, Victorian card backs. The Truthsayer deck is printed in cyanotype blue, like a seer's blueprints. The Be'lakor deck is still being made, in frames taken from summoning circles and burned grimoire pages.

The art on the cards is mostly paintings, prints and engravings by old masters, and every card carries a credit line naming the artist and the work.
